Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to apply?
Applying and screening are free. There is a single, one-off €500 processing fee, charged once your application is shortlisted for an employer. It covers document verification, work-permit filing and relocation coordination. We never charge a placement fee on top of this, and you should be cautious of anyone who asks for money before an employer has shown interest.
What exactly does the €500 processing fee cover?
The €500 is a one-off processing fee that covers verification of your documents and qualifications, preparation and filing of your salaried-worker permit application, liaison with the Luxembourg immigration authorities, and coordination of your housing and arrival. It is the only fee you pay us — there is no recurring charge and no commission taken from your salary.
Do I need to speak French, German or Luxembourgish?
It depends on the role. Many positions in logistics, IT, cleaning and international hospitality operate in English. Roles involving customers or public services may require French or German — the language level is always stated in the job description.
Will you help me get a work permit?
Yes. Once you have a signed offer, we guide you through the salaried-worker (travailleur salarié) permit application, help you prepare the document file and liaise with the Luxembourg immigration authorities. This support is included in the €500 processing fee.
Which documents do I need to prepare?
A current CV, your passport or national ID, academic or professional certificates, and proof of experience where relevant. Some employers may also request a police clearance certificate and a medical certificate.
How long does the process take?
Approximately 1½ months (about 6 weeks) from application to arrival for most candidates. This includes screening, interviews, payment of the €500 processing fee, the permit application and visa processing. Cases needing extra document checks can take a little longer.
Can my family come with me?
Once you hold a valid residence permit and meet the income requirements, you may be eligible to apply for family reunification for your spouse and dependent children.
